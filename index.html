OpenLogic

A logic circuit simulator that runs entirely in your browser. Nothing is uploaded anywhere — your circuits are plain JSON files on your own disk.

Building

  • Click a part in the left tray, then click the canvas to drop it. Hold Shift while placing to keep laying down more of the same part.
  • Drag from one pin to another to wire them up. Either direction works.
  • One output can feed many inputs. Several outputs may share one input — that's what Tri-State is for.
  • Click a toggle switch or hold a push button to drive the circuit while it runs.

Signals

  • Grey is 0, green is 1.
  • Dashed means floating (Z) — nothing is driving that net. Gates read a floating input as 0.
  • Red means two outputs are fighting over one net.

Saving

  • Download writes a .json file you can keep, email or commit to git.
  • Open loads one back, or just drag the file onto the canvas.
  • Your work is also kept in this browser's local storage as a crash net. Clearing site data erases it — download anything you care about.

Notes

  • All flip-flops trigger on the rising clock edge.
  • D flip-flop PRE and CLR are active high, so an unconnected pin does nothing.
  • Gate input counts are editable — select a gate and change Inputs in the panel on the right.
